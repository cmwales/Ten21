# Platform Overview & Strategic Direction

- Official Product Brand: Ten21 (integrates signature "1021" numeric anchor with a domain-agnostic nod to tenancy across residential, commercial, storage, and HOAs).
- Master Platform Domain: ten21.io (single unified domain for public web marketing, portal routing, and backend API endpoints).

## Product Vision & Strategic Positioning

- Domain-Agnostic Flexibility: Designed with domain-neutral terminology and flexible workflows to seamlessly support small landlords, multi-family residential properties, commercial suites, self-storage facilities, and HOAs without locking the platform into a single market segment.
- Lean & Low-Cost Philosophy: Eliminates enterprise software bloat to launch quickly with essential core management tools while maintaining ultra-low operational costs.

## What the Platform Does (V1 Landlord MVP Scope)

- Property, Unit & Lease Setup: Configure flexible property structures, track unit occupancy, define lease terms, and onboard occupants via automated email invitations.
- Rent Roll & Payment Logging: Log charges alongside manual payment records (Cash, Check, Zelle, Venmo, Direct Deposit) with real-time balance tracking.
- Maintenance & Asset Dispatch: Enable occupants to report issues with direct S3 photo attachments, allowing managers to prioritize repairs and dispatch work orders to vendors.
- Multi-Role Oversight: Provide distinct, secure interfaces tailored specifically for Property Managers, Property Owners, Occupants/Tenants, Vendors, and Board Members.

## User Experience & Design Standards

- Single-Action Simplicity: Every screen centers around one primary call-to-action so users never struggle with navigation.
- Mobile & Touch First: Responsive card-stack layouts engineered specifically for effortless phone and tablet navigation.
- Disability Accessible: Built to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards featuring high-contrast themes, visible focus rings, and screen-reader support.
- Multi-Language Support: Localized interface supporting English (en-US), US Spanish (es-US), and Canadian French (fr-CA).

## Future Direction (Phase 2 & Beyond)

- Automated Payments: Integrated tokenized ACH and credit card auto-pay processing.
- HOA Governance Suite: Board voting/elections, architectural alteration (ARC) approvals, and meeting quorum management.
- Double-Entry Accounting: Statutory assessment ledgers, automated late-fee tracking, and financial reporting.
