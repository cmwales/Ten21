## 2. Phase Execution Log

- **Phase 0 Completed:** Locked in .NET 9 + Angular stack, single shared database with EF Core filters & RLS, ASP.NET Core Identity with self-hosted Data Protection, tokenized ACH payment security, 9-tier role taxonomy, and PMC parent organization context switching.
- **Phase 4 Completed (out of roadmap order, ahead of Phase 1–3):** Built the Angular standalone SPA, Tailwind design tokens, `@ngx-translate` (en-US/es-US/fr-CA), a JWT `AuthService` + refresh-and-retry interceptor, and functional route guards enforcing the Tenant financial/ledger hard-block at the UI layer — pulled forward because Phase 1–3's backend work has no way to be manually exercised or demoed without a working login and UI shell first. See `User_Stories_Phase_0.md`'s sibling frontend stories (US-00, US-10–US-13) for the detailed record.
- **Phase 5 Completed:** Sprint covering US-14 (Workspace Registration & Onboarding), US-15 (Google OAuth & Post-Login Profile Completion), US-16 (Email Activation & Self-Service Password Recovery), US-17 (Email 6-Digit OTP Dual Authentication), and US-18 (Bot Defense & Registration Rate Limiting). Full acceptance criteria and design decisions in `User_Stories_Phase_5.md`. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, before the next branch was cut. A follow-up fix later removed TOTP/authenticator-app support in favor of email-only 2FA — see that doc's US-17 section.
- **Sprint 3 Completed:** Sprint covering US-19 (Unified Single Property & Unit Form Editor), US-20 (Property & Unit List View with Real-Time Search & Pagination), US-21 (In-Memory Bulk CSV/XLSX Property & Unit Importer), and US-22 (Conditional Payment-Based Property Deletion). Full acceptance criteria and design decisions in `User_Stories_Sprint_3.md`. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, same workflow as Phase 5. `Property`/`Unit` are now real entities (`DATA_MODEL.md` still needs a follow-up pass to document their schema formally). US-22's payment check is a deliberate placeholder (always `false`) pending Phase 1's payment ledger — see that story's section in `User_Stories_Sprint_3.md`. A same-day follow-up flattened `Property`/`Unit` into one entity after tester feedback — see `User_Stories_Sprint_3.md`'s "Flatten Property/Unit" addendum.
- **Sprint 4 Completed:** Sprint covering US-23 (Tenant Profile Directory), US-24 (Zero-Token Tenant Welcome & Provisioning), and US-25 (Tenant Access & Directory Privacy). Full acceptance criteria and design decisions in `User_Stories_Sprint_4.md`. New entities `ResidentProfile`/`EmergencyContact`; `ApplicationUser.MustChangePassword` plus a new `AuthController` login gate; `Property.AllowTenantDirectory`. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, same workflow as Sprint 3/Phase 5.
- **Sprint 5 Completed:** Sprint covering US-26 (Portfolio Expansion), US-27 (Switch-Context Test Coverage), and US-28 (Workspace Switcher UI). New `ITenantStampOverride` mechanism for stamping a brand-new workspace's rows during expansion; the pre-existing `POST /api/organization/switch-context` endpoint (Phase 0/US-04) finally got real multi-tenant data and test coverage to exercise it. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, same workflow as Sprint 3/4.
- **Sprint 6 Completed:** Sprint covering US-29 (Unit Tiers, Groups & Matrix Editor), US-30 (Lease Terms & Recurring Add-On Dues Schedule), US-31 (Ad-Hoc Manual Charges & Fines Engine), and US-32 (Pro-Rated Move-In Invoice & Lease Expiration Alerts). New entities `UnitTier`/`UnitGroup` (Property gained `UnitGroupId`/`UnitTierId`), `Lease`/`LeaseRecurringCharge`, `ManualCharge`. The matrix editor became a portfolio-wide `/properties/matrix` page rather than nesting under `/properties/:id`, since Sprint 3's flatten refactor already made `:id` a single door with no parent "property" left to host a multi-row matrix under. Pro-rated move-in charges reuse `ManualCharge` rather than a separate `ProRatedCharge` table (functionally identical open ledger line items). `PropertyManager` gained `Permissions.Property.Read` and `Permissions.Lease.Manage`/`Permissions.Ledger.Write` — the last one a deliberate widening beyond `BUSINESS_RULES`' original HOA-governance-flavored restriction, since this phase's V1 Landlord MVP has no separate Property Owner actively posting charges. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, same workflow as prior sprints.
- **A same-day tester-feedback fix (between Sprint 6 and Sprint 7):** `MoveOutNoticeDate` moved from `Lease` to `Property` (no one cares which one of several co-tenants gave notice, only when the unit needs a new occupant — same "billed to the unit, not the person" principle as `ManualCharge`), `ManualCharge.ResidentId` removed entirely, and `ManualCharge.PaidDate` added (later superseded by Sprint 7's `PaymentTransaction.PaymentDate`, see below).
- **Sprint 7 Completed:** Sprint covering US-33 (Unit Account Statement UI & Dynamic Balance Engine), US-34 (Multi-Tender Manual Payment Entry & Statutory Waterfall Allocation), US-35 (Charge Editing, Voiding & Line-Item Adjustment Engine), and US-36 (Hierarchical Property & Workspace Ledgers). `ManualCharge` renamed to `Charge` and extended to cover rent too (`Category`/`AllocationPriority`/`IsStatutoryLocked`/`Status`), since there's no automated recurring-billing engine yet and a PM manually posts every charge each period. New entities `PaymentTransaction`, `PaymentAllocation` (the many-to-many application of a payment to one or more charges), and `ChargeAdjustment` (a signed credit/debit correction with a mandatory reason, the only way to adjust a charge once a payment has been allocated to it). See `DATA_MODEL.md` §3 for the full schema and `BUSINESS_RULES.md` §4 for the statutory waterfall order. Balance is always computed at read time from these rows, never stored. `/properties/:id/ledger` (US-33) doubles as the per-property ledger in this codebase's flattened Property model (one row = one door, no separate Unit entity); `/ledger` (US-36) is the new portfolio-wide rollup above it, replacing the Phase 0 placeholder that used to live at that route. A same-day correction mid-sprint added a required `PaymentTransaction.ResidentProfileId` — money belongs to a specific human payee (for refund routing, co-tenant attribution, and per-resident history), even though `Charge` itself stays unit-scoped. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, same workflow as prior sprints; the resident-attribution correction got its own dedicated branch/merge, same pattern as the tester-feedback fix above.
- **Sprint 8 Completed:** Sprint covering US-37 (Overpayment Credit Store & Manual Drawdown Engine), US-38 (Payment Reversals & Atomic Cross-Property Reallocation), US-39 (Security Deposit Settlement & Refund Disbursement Engine), and US-40 (Exportable PDF Tenant Statements & Payment Receipts). New entities `CreditAllocation` (the deposit-money-equivalent of `PaymentAllocation`, drawing a payment's retained `UnallocatedAmount` down against outstanding charges), `RefundTransaction` (append-only cash-out of a resident's credit or deposit balance), `SecurityDeposit` and `DepositSettlementAllocation` (escrow money kept deliberately separate from `Charge`/`PaymentTransaction` so it never reads as rental income). `PaymentTransaction` gained `UnallocatedAmount` (the leftover-credit balance introduced by Sprint 7's waterfall, now actually drawn down), `Status`/`ReversalReason`/`ReallocatedToId` (a reversed payment un-links its allocations and restores the affected charges to Unpaid automatically, since balance is always computed live). Per explicit direction, credit drawdown ("Apply Credits to Charges") and deposit settlement are single PM-triggered buttons, not a background billing worker — no recurring/anchor-date engine exists in this codebase yet (Phase 2). PDF statements/receipts (US-40) use QuestPDF (Community license, free under $1M annual revenue) behind a new `IPdfService` seam; both endpoints return inline `application/pdf` bytes with no `Content-Disposition: attachment`, and the frontend just embeds them in an `<iframe>` at `/properties/:id/ledger/pdf` rather than building a second HTML rendering of the same statement/receipt data. See `DATA_MODEL.md` §4 for the full schema and `BUSINESS_RULES.md` §5 for the credit/deposit/reversal rules. A unit test caught a real Balance-formula bug mid-sprint (a `DepositReturn` refund was being double-counted as if it were an `OverpaymentRefund`) — fixed before merging; see that story's section for detail. Each story was built on its own branch and merged into `main` once its own build/tests were green, same workflow as prior sprints.
